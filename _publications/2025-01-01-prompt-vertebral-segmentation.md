This paper presents a prompt-guided segmentation framework for osteoporotic vertebral compression fractures (OVCFs) in spinal radiographs. We designed algorithms to improve detection robustness by integrating anatomical priors and explored foundation model applications in medical imaging to enhance generalizability. The proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in vertebral segmentation for OVCF patients, including challenging cases with implants such as vertebroplasty.
