I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence Technology at [National Taipei University of Technology (NTUT)](https://www.ntut.edu.tw/), Taipei, Taiwan. My thesis focuses on prompt-guided foundation segmentation models for accurate vertebral segmentation in osteoporotic vertebral compression fractures (OVCFs). I work in the Hyperspectral Imaging and Biomedical Research Lab under the supervision of Dr. Hsiao-Chi Li and Dr. Hsin-Han Chiang.

I also serve as a Research Assistant at Taipei Medical University Hospital, where I collaborate on clinical imaging projects. Prior to my master's studies, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from National Yunlin University of Science and Technology.

## Research Interests

- **Trustworthy AI**: Building reliable and interpretable AI systems
- **Medical Imaging**: Segmentation, diagnosis, and analysis of clinical images
- **Foundation Models**: Adapting large-scale pre-trained models to specialized tasks
- **Multimodal & Multitask Learning**: Integrating diverse data sources and tasks
